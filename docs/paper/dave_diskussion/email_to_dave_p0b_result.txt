Two more numbers that explain the tail, or rather un-explain it: mean vector

indistinguishable from position 130000 (7.57e-03) or 125500 (8.44e-03).

My reading: the tail is not a codec error at those positions. The logits are
sensitive there; the K/V is normal. That also means no per-vector correction
at the same error level can fix it - only a lower error everywhere (int8 is
22.9 dB better in vector MSE), more bits at specific cells, or a different read
path.

One limitation I want to be explicit about: the 18 positions come from the
windowed P0a arm. The pure q4lm32 arm writes no logits on the batched path, so
I have its KV error there but not its own logit tail. That is the remaining
control: a pure 16/16 run without the window, same 6,144 positions. It decides
whether the P0a tail is a codec effect or a read-path effect (exact window plus
q4 bulk). It changes no default.

Decision per the pre-registered rule: no 18-byte build, no 16-byte format, no
